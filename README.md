# Stand

A practice app for musicians that runs on iPad (and any modern browser) and works offline.

- **Scores** – import PDFs or photos of your part, turn pages by tapping the edges, swiping or with a Bluetooth page-turner pedal. Two pages side by side in landscape, warm or night paper, Apple Pencil markings (pen, highlighter, eraser, undo).
- **Metronome** – sample-accurate clicks, accents per beat, subdivisions, 6/8 · 7/8 · 9/8 · 12/8, tap tempo, five sounds. **Speed trainer** raises (or lowers) the tempo smoothly every beat or in steps every few bars. **Silent bars** to train your inner pulse.
- **Tuner** – chromatic, adjustable reference A (400–466 Hz).
- **Drone** – any note with an optional pure fifth and lower octave, three sounds.
- **Recorder** – record while reading the score (with optional count-in), loop a passage, slow it down to 50% with the pitch unchanged, add a soft room / hall / church reverb, and export a WAV of what you hear.
- Practice time is logged per score and per day.

Everything is stored on the device. Nothing is uploaded.

## Install on iPad
1. Open the app link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the Share button › **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Stand from the Home Screen. Allow the microphone the first time you use the tuner or recorder.
