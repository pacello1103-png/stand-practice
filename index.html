                        Writing

                        With the Apple Pencil, the first touch on a score opens the markings tools (it writes nothing); then write. Finger gestures still turn pages.

                        Turn pages by winking

                        Wink your right eye for the next page, your left eye to go back. Normal blinks are ignored.

                        Available in the Stand app for iPad (it uses the front camera; nothing is recorded or saved).

                        Turn pages by listening Beta

                        Stand follows your playing and turns the page in the middle of the last bar. Works best with clearly printed parts. This is new, so keep a pedal or a tap ready; tap the sides or use a pedal at any time.

                        When the page turns
                        Bar before the lastLast bar

                        The front camera sees roughly where on the page you are looking, and Stand uses it as a hint alongside the sound, to keep its place and to find it again. When you play by heart or look away, it notices and goes by the sound alone. Look at a few dots once to set it up. Nothing is recorded or saved.

                        Turn pages by tapping the stand

                        A firm knock on the iPad or its music stand turns to the next page. Playing and bowing do not count.

                        Available in the Stand app for iPad (it uses the motion sensors; nothing is recorded).

                        Backup

                        No backup yet

                        Deleting Stand deletes everything inside it. A backup keeps it as files in iCloud Drive, so you can bring it back on this or any iPad.

                        Everything goes in the Stand folder of this iPad (Files › On My iPad › Stand, with Stand’s picture), already in order. Nothing to choose:

                        • ScoresEvery score as a PDF, in the folders of your library
                        • RecordingsYour recordings and videos (the new ones, each time)
                        • BackupsYour whole library, to bring it all back (the three newest stay)
                        • DiagnosticsWhat went wrong, if anything did (to send to help)
                        What goes in the backup

                        In the sheet that opens, choose Save to Files, then iCloud Drive (a folder called Stand is a good place).

                          Automatic backup

                          When it is time and something has changed, Stand makes the backup by itself and asks you once to save it: one tap.

                          Where it goes: the first time, in the sheet choose Save to Files › iCloud Drive, make a folder called Stand and save there. After that the sheet opens in the Stand folder by itself.

                          Bring back

                          Choose the backup file in iCloud Drive. If a backup has several files, select all of them together.
