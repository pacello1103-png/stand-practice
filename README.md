# Stand

A practice app for musicians that runs on iPad (and any modern browser) and works offline.

- **Find scores on IMSLP** – type a composer and a piece; Stand finds it, ranks every edition by downloads and marks the best one for your instrument. Downloaded files get their title and composer automatically when imported.
- **Scores** – PDFs or photos, page turns by tapping, swiping or a Bluetooth pedal, two pages in landscape, half-page turns, warm or night paper, Apple Pencil markings.
- **Metronome** – accents per beat, subdivisions, compound meters, tap tempo, a speed trainer that raises the tempo smoothly or in steps, and silent bars.
- **Tuner** – precise to a fraction of a cent from low E1 to C7, intonation trace over the last 8 seconds, open strings in pure fifths, equal / just / Pythagorean targets.
- **Harmonics** – see the overtones of the note you play and hear them one by one or together.
- **Scales** – any key, 13 scale and arpeggio types, 1–4 octaves, equal, just or Pythagorean tuning, soft tone, optional drone and click.
- **Drone** – root, fifth, major or minor chord in pure intervals.
- **Recorder** – audio or video while reading the score, count-in, loop a passage, slow down to 50% without changing pitch, soft room / hall / church reverb, export.

Everything is stored on the device. Nothing is uploaded.

## Install on iPad
1. Open https://pacello1103-png.github.io/stand-practice/ in **Safari**.
2. Tap Share › **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Stand from the Home Screen. Allow the microphone (and camera for video) the first time.
