# Stand

A practice app for musicians that runs on iPad (and any modern browser) and works offline.

- **Find scores** – type a piece; Stand finds it on IMSLP and marks the best edition for your instrument. Tap Download and it opens straight in your library (in the app). Links to buy good editions (Henle, Bärenreiter, Stretta, Sheet Music Plus).
- **Scan** pages with the camera, or import PDFs and photos; title and composer are read automatically.
- **Scores** – tap, swipe, pedal or **wink** page turns (right eye forward, left eye back, Face ID iPads), half-page turns, pedal jumps, bookmarks, pinch zoom, trim margins, delete pages (tap to pick, put back any time), export PDF with or without markings.
- **Markings** – smooth Apple Pencil pen, two favourite colours always one tap away, highlighter, music symbols, text, hold at the end of a line to make it straight, an eraser that erases only what it touches, Pencil double-tap for the eraser, lasso to move, resize, recolour or duplicate, undo/redo across everything, and a smart eraser that removes printed marks from the score itself.
- **Notebook paper** – staves for every clef and ensemble, guitar tab, lined, dotted, grid and plain.
- **Listen** – choose the performer and open the piece on YouTube, Spotify or Apple Music; save the recordings you like; add an audio file to a score and play along with it, slower without pitch change and with an A–B loop.
- **Practice tracker** – daily goal, streak, week/month/year charts, time per piece, notes per day, an 18-week calendar.
- **Metronome** – accents, subdivisions, compound meters, tap tempo, smooth or stepped speed trainer, silent bars.
- **Tuner** – stable and precise, with intonation trace, equal / just / Pythagorean targets, and **Your intonation**: a map of which notes you tend to play sharp or flat.
- **Harmonics** – listens and tells you which natural harmonic you are playing and where to touch the string; hear it, hear the series, stop.
- **Drone** – soft, warm or organ sound; root, fifth, octave, open fifths, major or minor; walks the circle of fifths in time with the metronome.
- **Scales** – any key, 13 types, 1–4 octaves, three tunings, with the metronome in sync.
- **Recorder** – audio or video (recorded natively on iPad) while reading, loop, slow down without pitch change, soft reverb.
- **Blue or black** accent, light or dark, full backup and restore.

Everything is stored on the device. Nothing is uploaded.

## Install on iPad

**As an app (cable, Sideloadly)**
1. Download `Stand.ipa` from the latest release: https://github.com/pacello1103-png/stand-practice/releases/latest
2. Connect the iPad to your computer, open Sideloadly, drop in `Stand.ipa`, enter your Apple ID and press Start.
3. On the iPad: Settings › General › VPN & Device Management › trust your Apple ID. With a free Apple ID, re-sign every 7 days (your scores and recordings stay).
4. iOS 16+: turn on Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode once.

**As a web app (no computer)**
1. Open https://pacello1103-png.github.io/stand-practice/ in Safari.
2. Share › Add to Home Screen.

A new `Stand.ipa` is built automatically on every change.
