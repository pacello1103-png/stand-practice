How it works

  1. Type a composer and a piece. Stand searches IMSLP, the free library of public-domain scores.
  2. Open the piece. Stand ranks every edition by how often musicians downloaded it and marks the best one for your instrument.
  3. Tap Download, then accept IMSLP’s terms on their page. Back in Stand, tap Import score and pick the file from Downloads. Title and composer fill in by themselves.
