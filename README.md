# Tutti

Multi-camera recording with iPhones and iPads.

- **Camera** — open Tutti on every iPhone/iPad that films and tap *Camera*. It finds the director over Wi‑Fi (no router needed).
- **Director** — on the iPad, tap *Director*: you see every camera live, tap one to put it on air (with a cut, dissolve, dip, wipe, push or blur), and *Record all* starts every camera on the same frame.
- When you stop, every camera sends its full-quality file (4K HEVC + 24‑bit sound) and the recording opens in the editor, already cut the way you switched.
- **Editor** — every angle plays in step; tap an angle while listening to cut there. Drag cuts on the timeline, change transitions, undo.
- **Picture** — all cameras matched to one reference (brightness and colour), steady brightness, per-camera exposure/warmth, zoom & reframing with Lanczos scaling and detail recovery.
- **Sound** — main microphone + room microphones (aligned to the sample by listening, clock drift corrected), tone, compression, hall/church/studio space, loudness to −14/−18/−23 LUFS with a limiter.
- **Import** — videos filmed elsewhere (or a separate audio recorder) are lined up by their sound.

## Install

1. Download `Tutti.ipa` from the latest *Tutti* release (tags `tutti-1.x`).
2. Install with Sideloadly on each device (same as Stand and Duo).
